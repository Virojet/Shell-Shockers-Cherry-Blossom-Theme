## Install

1. **Get Tampermonkey** for your browser: [Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tampermonkey/dhdgffkkebhmkfjojejmpbldmpobfkfo) · [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/tampermonkey/iikmkjmpaadaobahmlepeloendndfphd) · [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/tampermonkey/) · [Opera GX & others](https://www.tampermonkey.net/) (or use [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/))
2. **[Click here to install the Cherry Blossom Theme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Virojet/Shell-Shockers-Cherry-Blossom-Theme/main/Shell-Shockers-Cherry-Blossom.user.js)**, then press **Install** on the page that opens
3. **Reload [shellshock.io](https://shellshock.io)**

> [!TIP]
> **Theme not showing?** Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera need one extra switch: open your browser's **Extensions** page → **Tampermonkey** → **Details** → turn on **Allow User Scripts**. Then reload the game. [Step by step for each browser](https://github.com/Virojet/Shell-Shockers-Better-Hud-Mod#allow-user-scripts)

## What it changes

- **Menus, panels, tabs, popups and text fields** turn pink and plum, on a dark plum background.
- **Buttons** get a soft plum finish that lightens when you hover them.
- **The loading screen** fades from plum to blossom pink, with a pink progress bar and a "✿ Cherry Blossom ✿" title.
- **Petals fall** gently across the screen. They hide while you're aiming in a match, so they never get in your way.
- **Team colors stay the same**, so you can still tell teams apart.

It's cosmetic only: it adds a stylesheet and the falling petals, and has no account or network code. It **updates by itself** through Tampermonkey. To turn it off, switch it off in Tampermonkey and reload the game.
